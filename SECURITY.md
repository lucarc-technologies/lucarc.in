# Security Policy

At **Lucarc** (`lucarc.in`), security and multi-tenant data isolation are paramount. We take all security reports seriously and are committed to investigating and resolving potential vulnerabilities promptly.

---

## Supported Versions

We actively maintain and provide security patches for the following versions:

| Version | Supported |
|---|---|
| **main / latest** | :white_check_mark: Active |
| **ClearDays v1.x** | :white_check_mark: Active |
| **PrepForge v1.x** | :white_check_mark: Active |
| Older releases | :x: Not supported |

---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

> [!CAUTION]
> **Do NOT create a public GitHub Issue for security vulnerabilities.**

If you discover a potential security vulnerability in any Lucarc product, library, or website, please report it privately:

1. **Email our Security Team**:  
   [**security@lucarc.in**](mailto:security@lucarc.in)
2. **What to include in your email**:
   - Type of issue (e.g., Cross-Site Scripting, SQLi, IDOR, Authentication Bypass).
   - Affected URLs, routes, or component filenames.
   - Step-by-step reproduction instructions or proof-of-concept (PoC) scripts.
   - Potential impact of the vulnerability.
3. **Response SLA**:
   - We will respond to your initial report **as soon as possible** (typically within 24–48 business hours).
   - We will provide regular updates as we triage, verify, and remediate the issue.
   - We will coordinate public disclosure timelines with you once a fix is released.

---

## Safe Harbor & Responsible Disclosure

We value researchers who act in good faith. We agree not to pursue legal action against researchers who:
- Engage in testing without harming our users, customers, or production availability.
- Avoid accessing, modifying, or deleting private tenant data.
- Give us reasonable time to remediate reported vulnerabilities before public disclosure.

Thank you for helping keep Lucarc and our developer community safe!
