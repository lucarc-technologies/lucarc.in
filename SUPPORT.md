# Community Support & Help

Need help with **Lucarc**, our products (**ClearDays**, **PrepForge**), or integrating our open-source tools?

---

## 1. Ask a Question in GitHub Discussions

We use **GitHub Discussions** as our central hub for community Q&A, feature requests, architecture discussions, and show-and-tell.

- **[Visit GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/lucarc-technologies/lucarc.in/discussions)**
- **Categories**:
  - **General**: Open conversations about software engineering and SaaS product design.
  - **Ideas**: Share feature requests or product suggestions.
  - **Q&A**: Ask technical questions about setup, architecture, or configuration.
  - **Announcements**: Follow Lucarc releases, roadmap milestones, and community updates.
  - **Show and Tell**: Show what you have built using Lucarc tools or templates!

---

## 2. Direct Email Support

If you have a private question, partnership inquiry, or need commercial support:

- **Email Support**: [**support@lucarc.in**](mailto:support@lucarc.in)
- **Careers & Hiring**: [**careers@lucarc.in**](mailto:careers@lucarc.in)
- **Security Reports**: [**security@lucarc.in**](mailto:security@lucarc.in) *(do not use Discussions for security)*

---

## 3. Bug Reports & Feature Requests

- Found a confirmed bug? [Open a Bug Report](https://github.com/lucarc-technologies/lucarc.in/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) in our issue tracker.
- Want to propose a new feature? [Open a Feature Request](https://github.com/lucarc-technologies/lucarc.in/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
